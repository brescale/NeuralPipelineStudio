# 🤝 Credits & Acknowledgements / Ringraziamenti

**Neural Pipeline Studio** stands on the shoulders of giants. This suite brings together the incredible work of graphics programmers, modders, and open-source engineers across the PC gaming ecosystem. We extend our deepest gratitude and recognition to the following creators and projects:

---

### 🎨 ReShade & Crosire
- **Project:** [ReShade](https://reshade.me)
- **Creator:** Crosire and the ReShade Development Team
- **Contribution:** The core advanced post-processing runtime, DirectX/Vulkan hook interposers, and flexible .addon64 runtime architecture that makes custom pipeline stages possible.

---

### 🌈 RenoDX HDR & Tensor Synthesis
- **Project:** [RenoDX](https://github.com/clshortfuse/renodx)
- **Creator:** clshortfuse and contributors
- **Contribution:** Groundbreaking native HDR volumetric color grading, tone-mapping transformations, and neural tensor-assisted lighting synthesis.

---

### 🌉 OptiScaler Universal Upscaling Bridge
- **Project:** [OptiScaler](https://github.com/cdozdil/OptiScaler)
- **Creator:** cdozdil and contributors
- **Contribution:** The revolutionary open-source middleware that translates DLSS, FSR 2.2/3.1, and XeSS 1.3 calls, enabling universal upscaling compatibility across all graphics cards (NVIDIA RTX/GTX, AMD Radeon, and Intel Arc).

- **Contribution:** Deep Learning Super Sampling (DLSS 2.x, 3.x, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5, 5.0), Streamline open interposer framework, and DLSS-NR (Neural Reconstruction) AI denoisers.
